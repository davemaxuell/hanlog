## Writing notes

The six approved mission seeds and first two sample challenges are unchanged. The catalog uses friendly examples, not claims that these are the newest expressions. Six use-mode keywords stay fixed while the surrounding sentence may change: 진짜, 화이팅, 대박, 혹시, 헐, 소확행.

Romanization was drafted against the [National Institute of Korean Language rules](https://www.korean.go.kr/front_eng/roman/roman_01.do); please check it alongside the spoken Korean. A [2026 National Institute discussion](https://www.korean.go.kr/front/onlineQna/onlineQnaView.do?mn_id=&pageIndex=1&qna_seq=336085) identifies 소확행 as a shortened expression. These references do not replace this review.
