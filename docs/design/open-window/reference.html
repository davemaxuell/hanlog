01 / PrinciplesFive governing rules

Friendly through care.

Warmth comes from readable content, useful feedback, and space to settle. A disciplined set of shapes gives that warmth a recognizable character.

1.Give the margin a job.

On broad layouts, put dates, times, and short markers in a narrow rail beside the content. On small screens, move labels above the group. Never sacrifice readable content to preserve the rail.

2.Use one quiet signpost.

A short geometric corner mark identifies the current section. Use at most one per view, paired with a text label. Its meaning stays consistent; it is never a repeating decoration.

3.Keep edges gently squared.

Use 6 px status badges, 8 px compact fields, 12 px buttons and cards, and 20 px expanded sheets. Underline the selected tab. Consistent geometry feels calm and dependable.

4.Let words and media breathe.

Place names, captions, and actions below media. Pair expressive serif titles with readable sans-serif body text. Give adjacent elements room before adding containers.

5.Make every state considerate.

Say what happened in plain language. Confirm success with a filled square, a check, and a word. Keep failed actions recoverable with the person’s input completely intact.

05 / DirectionRecorded standard

A distinct starting point.

Open Window is the recorded visual direction for future UI work. It pairs airy pastel sky (#D2F6FC), buttercream yellow (#FCFCC6), and strawberry/blush star accents with margin-led organization, gentle squared geometry, expressive serif headlines, and in-place state confirmation.

All four specimens above run purely on the Sky, Buttercream & Berry tokens defined in this system, ready to translate into product screens once audience and scope are confirmed.
